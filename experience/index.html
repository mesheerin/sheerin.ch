Michael E. Sheerin

Two decades of answers.

Strategy

Market entry. An exchange group’s new European venue had no German client yet. I built the FX sales and marketing campaign that won the first, then extended it to South Africa.

Launch. A regulatory technology start-up with no revenue. With the founder, I brought it to signed contracts with a benchmark administrator in Ireland and a fund manager in Malta.

Relaunch and wind-down. A stalled fitness studio with six employees. As operating investor, I wrote the business case, raised capital and ran the commercial side, then supported the orderly closure.

Execution

Trading book. Five years of market making in physical precious metals and swaps at two Swiss banks. I managed inventory, liquidity and balance sheet in gold, silver and platinum group metals, and directed a sourcing project with suppliers worldwide.

Infrastructure migration. Electronic trading relationships with key accounts and vendors in five countries. I moved them through a data centre retirement and relocation that lowered cost.

Chief of staff. A blockchain venture with a team across Europe and the Americas. I set up its reporting and secured grant funding. Later, part-time and interim, I held the same role in an owner-led business.

Risk and governance

Product approval. The FX, Rates and Credit business of a global systemically important bank, across the Investment Bank and Wealth Management. I advised on distribution and trading, and represented compliance on new-business committees.

Benchmark governance. The London auctions that set precious metals reference prices. I supervised a major bank’s participation.

Regulatory change. A custodian group’s European clearing broker unit, under MiFID and EMIR. I led the impact analysis that determined how the front office would comply.
