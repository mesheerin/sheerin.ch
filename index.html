I have priced risk, controlled it and carried it.

Owners and executives of SMEs and financial businesses face three questions, often at once.

Strategy. Where should the company go next? A market to enter, a line to build or one to close.

Execution. Who does the work? Each new requirement lands on the same small team, and time at the top is short.

Risk and governance. Can the firm show how it is run? Banks, counterparties and regulators want proof of ownership, oversight and conduct before they commit, and again before they renew.

I have handled all three from the trading desk, the control function and the operator’s seat.
