Three mandates, each with a defined scope and term.

Strategy. The options, the numbers and a recommendation. The result is a decision paper the owner or board can act on.

Execution. Chief of staff, business manager or interim operations lead. The result is a plan, a weekly update and the work done.

Risk and governance. The rules that apply, the controls that meet them and the evidence behind both. The result is a file that stands up to review.

Legal and audit opinions remain with your counsel and auditors.
